# The owner's preferences

Learned from the owner's corrections; apply them without being asked again.

- **App look: Abandoned bunker** (2026-10-30), chosen from three mockups (Night blizzard, Abandoned bunker, Bloodstained snow). Concrete grey, hazard stripes, rust accents, cards as paper notices clipped to a wall, typewriter fonts. The owner likes to see side-by-side mockups before a design choice. The login page stays neutral.
- **Post-apocalyptic means worn, not clean** (2026-10-30): notes look dirty and ragged (torn edges, stains, creases, grime), never fresh. Realism matters: the owner rejected hand-drawn zig-zag edges and a faint "coffee ring" as cheap indie-game looks; natural noise-based fraying and dirt across the whole sheet worked. Keep the fold crease. Paper stays light enough to read easily. No perforations or holes in the paper (tried, removed 2026-10-30). Revealing a card unfolds the note (three folds), as natural as possible. Buttons are stamped (Special Elite, uppercase): rust for the main action, gray for secondary ones like New game, Cancel and Sign out.
- **Note edges dirty and blackened** (2026-11-07): the torn edge of every note gets a near-black charred rim, soot fading about 15 px inward and uneven darker patches, following the actual tear (not the note's rectangle). The earlier edges looked too clean. Drawn as a filter-free SVG picture per note size (`tornEdgeImage` in torn-edge.ts), approved on the preview.
- **Forgiving for players** (2026-10-30): never punish a misclick. Choices can be changed or undone until the player confirms (e.g. options: tap another to switch, tap again to undo, then Done). Think of new players. Anything that ends something (sign out, new game) asks first with Yes and Cancel.
- **Buttons feel physical** (2026-10-30): every button sits raised with a shadow edge and sinks in while pressed; the action runs on release. They look like old, scuffed, slightly rusty metal plates (rust-red main action, steel secondary, tin for Not Triggered), never new or shiny (2026-10-31). Worn metal is patchy with random scratches; even, parallel streaks read as wood and are rejected. The steel buttons' brushed look is approved as is. The card's option buttons stay plain paper buttons (rust outline), not metal.
- **Done just appears** (2026-10-31): after picking an option, the hint and Done simply appear at the bottom of the card, with no line or crease above them. Tried and rejected: growing the card with an unfold and a separate strip folding out from behind (looked bad).
- **Alignment matters** (2026-10-31): notes hang straight (no tilt) so all their edges line up vertically. Hazard stripes above the options: black only (yellow see-through), as wide as the text, slanting like the top bar, and ending in matching small black triangles at both ends.
- **Creases never move** (2026-10-31): creases stay where they are. Since 2026-11-01 the card never changes size (the space for the hint and Done is there from the start), so this holds by itself.
- **Plates kept lean** (2026-10-31): rust-red and tin plates have grimy patches, pits and scratches only; no rust patches on rust-red, no light patches on tin or Cancel.
- **Speed over animation** (2026-11-01): the app must respond instantly and work smoothly on the owner's iPhone (Safari). All animations were removed (turns, unfold, refold, grow/shrink); every button goes straight to the next note. The animated version is kept as branch and tag `animation-backup`. A second backup, branch and tag `animation-backup-2` (2026-11-08, approved on the preview), is the current app with the paper animations back, meant as the base for the owner's private Flutter iPhone app. In it the paper stays torn and dirty through every turn, unfold and fold (flaps are slices of the card's torn paper, fold lines slightly rough), and no piece may ever go missing or show through: clip-paths are switched by timer, never animated, because Chrome on a 135 % scaled screen cut animated clips short (right side and bottom vanished). Test animations at fractional screen scaling too. Test new visual effects for cost on iPhone Safari: live SVG filters are slow there (the torn paper edge is now a precomputed clip-path outline, not a filter), so no live filters on notes, and nothing that makes a large note repaint or change size. The wall and the paper grain are SVG noise rendered once to WebP pictures (scripts/render-textures.mjs): no live SVG filters in backgrounds either (they made scrolling heavy, 2026-11-03). Visual fixes change only what was wrong: when fixing the corner triangles, the rest of the torn edges had to stay exactly as they were. No metal clip on the notes.
- **Bunker wall realism** (2026-11-01): grey concrete blocks (no metal plates or rivets) with grime at the joints and water streaks (approved, keep). Everything looks unmaintained for decades but real, never cartoony or patterned: rust only sporadic and small, where metal meets metal (couplings, clamps, bolts, hinges), never a coat; metal worn like the buttons (patchy grime, pits, random scratches), never parallel streaks (they read as wood); cracks angular and tapering, some as webs; no fake chip holes; no dings or dents anywhere (tried as ovals and as shaded hollows, both rejected 2026-11-01). Hazard bars top and bottom: worn painted steel (scratches, worn edges, pits, grime). Mounted objects (breaker boxes, vent) keep their grime on their own face so they stand off the wall; a flat sign may share grime with the wall.
- **Sound** (2026-11-03): narration in the Kokoro voice am_michael (Michael, American), generated locally. Sound switches in a steel switch box with levers and lamps at the top right: Narrate, Ambience (not "Storm"), Zombies (random ambient growls). Ambience must be gentle enough to run through a whole game: a howling storm was too harsh; now BigSoundBank Whistling of the Wind #1 at a low level, under the growls; zombie growls BigSoundBank Zombie #1-#7; all CC0. Play sound clips exactly as the original files: never re-encode, compress or add generated sound (a synthetic snow hiss was rejected); only the volume may change, and loops are blended in the player.
- **Intense music** (2026-11-07): a fifth lever "Intense music" between Music and Zombies, playing the owner's track zombie-music-intense2.mp3 (private data, replaced the first intense track). Music and Intense music are linked: only one can be on, switching one on switches the other off. Intense music comes in at full volume at once (no fade-in, preloaded while music plays); the normal Music keeps its gentle fade-in. Approved on the preview.
- **Screams** (2026-11-07): a sixth lever "Screams" after Zombies, for survivors dying. The owner uses it only together with Intense music in intense moments, so screams are set to be heard clearly over the Intense music (about 3 dB louder than it); a safety limiter on the output keeps overlaps from crackling. They start straight away, a new one every 1–2 s, overlapping and mixing, never the same voice twice at once. Four CC0 Freesound studio voices chosen by ear (candidates 2, 3, 17, 16 of 20; the hysterical screams, 14, were rejected), cut into single screams at even loudness (`prepare-sounds.py screams`). The owner judges sound by ear: send candidates as playable audio files at equal loudness, never a page of links; low-quality or uneven recordings get rejected. Spoken lines ("please no", "help me") are scarce in good CC0 quality.
- **Switch box: engraved nameplate, centred** (2026-11-07): chosen from three mockups (engraved plate, label tape, stencil). The SOUND label is an engraved aluminium nameplate with two screws, letters cut in and filled black (Barlow Condensed, served locally), and as scratched, dirty and worn as the other plates and buttons; the owner asks for that wear on every new label. The sound box sits centred at the top, above the notes (no longer top right).
