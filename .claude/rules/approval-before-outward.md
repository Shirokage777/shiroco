# Nothing goes out without a click

## Trigger
About to deploy to production, push to a branch other people use, send a message, publish anything, or delete or overwrite something.

## Action
- Show the owner exactly what will happen: the target, and the full content for anything another person will read. For app changes, that is the Vercel preview link.
- Wait for an explicit go ("go", "yes", "ship it"). A go covers only what you showed; a change after the go needs a new go.
- Drafts and previews are always fine; going live is never automatic.

## Done when
Every outward action had its own preview and go.
